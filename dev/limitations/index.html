Limitations and testing

Scientific limitations

  • No reference, no proof. Without external references, read-level information (quality scores, read counts per replicate, error models) or independent evidence (e.g. RT-PCR, morphology), MitoASV cannot definitively tell genuine mitochondrial variants from sequencing errors, NUMTs and chimeras. Recent NUMTs can be fully open-reading and biochemically ordinary; real variants can be rare and unusual.
  • Relative tests depend on groups. Comparative scores are only as good as the comparison groups. With species_col they are only as good as the species assignment: a misassigned sequence is compared with the wrong species (clustering within the species usually isolates it, which gives insufficient comparators rather than a false score). Connected components can chain divergent sequences; any fixed threshold can split a species or merge several. Groups that are too small give no scores (insufficient comparators) rather than unreliable ones.
  • ASVs are not independent. Related ASVs share ancestry, so conservation estimated from them is biased toward the sampled diversity. Identical sequences are collapsed and leave-one-out estimates are used, but closely related sequences still dominate. Henikoff weights reduce the effect without removing it.
  • Homology is assumed, not proven. Positions are compared only between equal-length sequences read in the same frame within a group (or columns of an aligned input). Sequences of different length are not compared without an alignment.
  • Hydrophobicity is weak evidence. Thresholds are not universal for ND2 or COI, and a plausible profile does not prove function.
  • Codon statistics. Nei–Gojobori pathway averaging is an approximation. Between closely related sequences dN/dS reflects polymorphism, not selection, and is off by default.
  • Concordance is indirect. Animal mtDNA does not normally recombine, but incomplete sampling, homoplasy, rate differences between genes, NUMTs and errors also create discordance. A discordant ASV is a candidate for checking (e.g. with UCHIME-type tools and read data), not a confirmed chimera.
  • Flags are diagnostics. Thresholds (3.5 robust units, Grantham ≥ 101, ...) are conventions recorded in the output. Change them to fit your data and report what you used.

Testing and simulated data

test/runtests.jl covers:

  • translation with table 5, ambiguous nucleotides, internal stops, partial sequences and invalid frames;
  • hydrophobicity, Grantham distances, synonymous/nonsynonymous classification and clustering;
  • conservation, robust scores and gene concordance;
  • missing values, small groups, identical sequences and zero-variance groups.

simulate_asv_dataset builds a simulated ND2–spacer–COI dataset (random open reading frames under table 5, not real sequences) with the following categories:

  • plausible variants;
  • a single conservative nonsynonymous change;
  • a radical substitution;
  • an internal stop;
  • a 1-nt deletion;
  • an ND2–COI recombinant;
  • a divergent but plausible lineage;
  • a second, unrelated species (species column: Simulatus alpha, Simulatus beta).

All simulated rows have simulated = true.
